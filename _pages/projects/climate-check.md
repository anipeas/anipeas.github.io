> ### CLIMATE CHECK (2025)

- <small> A Streamlit application for visualizing and analyzing global climate trends using historical weather data. The tool enables users to explore temperature patterns, precipitation levels, and long-term climate changes across locations worldwide. </small>

#### Data Sources

- <small> Leverages NOAA's Global Summary of the Month (GSOM) and Global Historical Climatology Network Daily (GHCND) datasets, providing access to comprehensive weather observations from thousands of stations globally. </small>

#### Key Features

- <small> **Temperature Analysis** - Visualize average, minimum, and maximum temperature trends over time for any location with available weather station data. </small>

- <small> **Precipitation Tracking** - Analyze rainfall and snowfall patterns to understand seasonal variations and long-term precipitation trends. </small>

- <small> **Historical Comparisons** - Compare weather patterns across different time periods to identify climate shifts and anomalies in specific regions. </small>
