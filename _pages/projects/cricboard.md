> ### CRICBOARD (2021)

- <small> Cricboard is a comprehensive cricket statistics platform designed to deliver advanced insights through a dynamic, interactive user interface. The platform was specifically developed to serve fantasy league participants and cricket enthusiasts seeking detailed statistical analysis and performance metrics. </small>
- <small> The web application demonstration below showcases the platform's core functionality and user experience. </small>

[ ![](/assets/images/cricboard_demo.gif) ](/assets/images/cricboard_demo.gif)

- <small> The platform achieved significant traction during IPL 2021, generating over 30,000 page views on launch day and maintaining consistent daily traffic of 1,000-2,000 visitors until the tournament's conclusion due to COVID-19 restrictions. </small>
- <small> The complete source code has been made publicly available in the [repository](https://github.com/Anirudh58/cricboard).  </small>
