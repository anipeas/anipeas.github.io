> ### PNEUMONIA DETECTION (2021)

- <small> Driven by a vision of democratizing healthcare through AI, this project explores the potential for providing accessible diagnostic tools in underserved regions where medical consultation is challenging. The goal is to develop AI-powered health services that can be deployed cost-effectively in remote areas. </small>
- <small> Implemented a convolutional neural network using the [Kaggle CoronaHack Chest X-Ray Dataset](https://www.kaggle.com/praveengovi/coronahack-chest-xraydataset) to distinguish between healthy and pneumonic chest X-rays. The model demonstrates effective classification capabilities, as shown in the test dataset samples and predictions below. </small>
